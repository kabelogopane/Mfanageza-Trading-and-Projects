# Mfanageza Trading and Projects
## Website handover guide

Welcome. This guide explains the Mfanageza Trading and Projects website in everyday language. You do not need a technical background to read it.

## 1. What the website is for

The website introduces Mfanageza Trading and Projects (Pty) Ltd to people who may want to learn about the company, ask about its services, or discuss working together.

It gives visitors a simple way to:
- Understand who the company is.
- Read about the company's main areas of work.
- Learn about the company's approach and values.
- See the contact locations supplied for the business.
- Contact the company by phone or WhatsApp.

## 2. What visitors will find

| Website area | What it tells visitors |
| --- | --- |
| Home | A short introduction to the company and what it stands for. |
| About us | When the company was established and the vision behind it. |
| Our services | The three main areas currently shown: skills development, trading and business services, and project support. |
| Our approach | The importance of listening, practical action and working with others. |
| Our locations | The Johannesburg and Virginia addresses supplied for the website. |
| Contact | The company phone number and a link to start a WhatsApp conversation. |

## 3. The look and feel

The website uses a modern blue design with bright accents, clear headings and photographs. It is designed to feel professional, welcoming and connected to learning, technology and opportunity.

The layout adjusts to different screen sizes, so visitors can use it on a computer, tablet or mobile phone.

## 5. Important note about training and accreditation

The website mentions skills development and a learning-focused mindset. It does not promise that the company is a university, TVET college or accredited training provider.

No specific course, qualification, intake date, fee or accreditation is advertised in the current version. These details should only be added after the company confirms them and provides the correct supporting information.

## 6. What is needed to complete the website

The company can help finish the website by providing or confirming:

1. The final company description and approved service list.

4. Real photographs of the team, work, projects or premises that the company has permission to use.

Visitors can select the phone number to call the company or use the WhatsApp link to open a conversation with the listed number.

The company should test both links on a mobile phone before sharing the website.
